# Phase 8 — Three boss identities

The Forge Saint, Thorn King and Bellless King now have individual original transparent two-frame sheets in `assets/characters/`. The left half is Phase 1; the right half is the awakened form. The final boss's crowned wings, the Forge Saint's hammer and furnace, and the Thorn King's spined roots create distinct outer silhouettes at gameplay zoom. The previous `boss.svg` remains a safe fallback if a PNG cannot be loaded, and the miniboss still uses that legacy asset.

`actors/enemy.gd` crops the appropriate half, switching halfway through the existing transformation window. Existing AI, health thresholds, attack timing, damage and hit windows are unchanged. A short forward lean/scale during windup, directional lunge during charge, and lowered/brighter posture during recovery expose existing combat states. The existing floor hazards, cone/line previews and attack gaps remain authoritative; brief bounded arcs accent transformation and openings.

The sheets were generated specifically for ASHEN VOW with GPT Image, using `references/art-direction.md` and `references/audio-motion.md` as direction; no third-party art was used. This phase does not replace music or sound: the existing boss and awakened music, warnings and impact sounds are already wired to the preserved combat timeline. Audio production across all seven weapon families is scheduled for Phase 11.

Validation uses `tests/phase8_runner.gd` for original textures, alpha/phase regions and transition/recovery behavior; `tests/phase8_visual.gd` for 18 rendered iPad-landscape touch-layout captures of the three bosses in Phase 1, windup, recovery, transformation and awakened windup/recovery. `tests/campaign_runner.gd` selects either branch guardian and records actual rendered Phase 1/2 combat frames for it and the final boss while completing each campaign with normal attacks. `tests/visual_runner.gd` preserves the general UI regression. The local Godot 4.5.1 Web export and CI browser smoke verify packaged runtime compatibility; the 1180×820 touch-layout captures verify iPad proportions. Physical iPad Safari remains to be tested on a device.
